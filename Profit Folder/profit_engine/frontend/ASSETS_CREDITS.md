# Aswenna Asset Attributions & Credits

The Aswenna Agricultural Decision Support System utilizes open cartographic boundaries and 3D asset frameworks under permissible open-source and Creative Commons licenses:

## 1. Geographic & Cartographic Data
- **geoBoundaries (LKA ADM2):** Run by the William & Mary geoLab. Provides comprehensive, open-license administrative district boundary vectors for Sri Lanka's 25 administrative districts.
  - License: Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL 1.0) / CC-BY 4.0.
  - Source: [geoBoundaries.org](https://www.geoboundaries.org/)
- **Department of Agriculture Sri Lanka (DOA):** Agro-ecological zones, cost of cultivation statistics, and market survey baselines.
